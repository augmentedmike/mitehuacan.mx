# Corporate & Chain Sponsors — Phase 1 Reality Check

*2026-07-20. Question: can large corporations (Chedraui, OXXO, etc.) be Phase 1
sponsors? Grounded in our DENUE layer (branch counts in the service area) plus
outside research on how each org actually buys local visibility. Companion to
business/marketing-plan.md §4 and the adversarial review.*

## The segmentation that matters: WHERE the decision lives

### Tier 1 — corporate BRAND, LOCAL decision-maker → these ARE Phase 1 targets

The pitch is identical to any local business; the owner is reachable in
Tehuacán; the brand logo on our map buys us credibility with everyone else.

- **Telcel distribuidores autorizados** (Grupo Accell et al.). Independent
  regional companies with their own stores, own marketing, commission-driven
  — 38 points of presence and a real incentive to be found. *Top corporate-
  flavored prospect in the whole list.*
- **Farmacias Similares franchisees.** National brand advertising is
  corporate (franchisees pay 1% into the co-op fund), but franchisees run
  complementary local promotion — a per-sucursal pin is exactly that.
- **Food franchise operators** (Domino's, Little Caesars, Italian Coffee —
  typically regional franchise groups with local managers who own a P&L).
- **Gasolinera groups** (often local families; destination businesses on
  corridors).
- **Caja Popular Mexicana** — cooperative with regional decision latitude;
  branches are genuine trip destinations for members.

**How to sell Tier 1:** exactly like business/marketing-plan.md §7, plus the multi-location
lever our contract system was built for: *paquete multi-sucursal* (e.g., all
5 Similares of one franchisee, price per location with a volume step-down).

### Tier 2 — centralized corporates: slow lane, one flagship attempt only

- **Chedraui (2 stores).** Real community-program culture; a store gerente
  can champion but budget lives at HQ. Realistic path: one-page proposal
  through the Tehuacán gerente to regional marketing ("your store as the
  named destination on the routes that serve it, X impressions/month,
  $ trivial"). Expect a 3–6 month cycle and treat any yes as a case-study
  win, not a revenue line. Cap effort: one proposal, quarterly follow-up.
- **Banks, Coppel, Walmart/Aurrera, Guadalajara, 3B/Neto, Waldos, Autozone:**
  centralized procurement, national media buying, or (3B/Neto) a no-ads
  doctrine. **Park them.** Do not spend field afternoons here in Phase 1.

### Tier 3 — the inversion: some "targets" are media SELLERS

**OXXO/FEMSA runs Retina Media** — its own retail-media network (8,000+
in-store screens, Spin Premia's ~23M users, programmatic via Place Exchange).
FEMSA is in the business of *selling* audience, not buying pins on someone
else's map. Implications:

1. OXXO will essentially never be a paying Phase 1 sponsor.
2. Keep OXXO as our **free seed layer** — 40 branches are genuine wayfinding
   landmarks; their pins make the map more useful and the marketplace look
   alive. Cost: zero. That's what the seed was for.
3. Long-term (Phase 3+), the interesting conversation with FEMSA-scale
   players is **audience partnership** (their promos surfaced to our riders),
   which requires DAU numbers we don't have yet. File away.
4. Same logic applies to Walmart Connect (Aurrera).

**Adjacent Tier-3 idea worth remembering:** consumer-brand **trade marketing**
(Coca-Cola, Bimbo, Modelo route-level budgets) sometimes funds hyperlocal
activations. Real money, but it buys *proof*, not promises — revisit when we
can show tens of thousands of route views/month.

## What corporates are actually worth in Phase 1

- **Navigation value, free:** Chedraui, Aurrera, Coppel, banks as named
  destination pins (unpaid, like OXXO) make the app better and the map look
  inhabited. Riders navigate BY these places. This costs nothing and sells
  the local businesses around them.
- **Credibility transfer:** "junto a Chedraui, sobre la Ruta 12" is sales
  language for the taquería next door.
- **Revenue:** comes from Tier 1 (franchise/distributor operators) and the
  local businesses that are the core of business/marketing-plan.md — not from HQ marketing
  departments in Monterrey.

## Phase 1 action list (fits inside existing corridor plan)

1. Identify the **Telcel distribuidor** group(s) behind the 38 points (Grupo
   Accell shows in DENUE) — one meeting, multi-sucursal paquete proposal.
2. Map which **Similares are franchises vs corporate sucursales** during
   normal corridor visits (ask at the counter; franchises get the pitch).
3. Franchise operators of Domino's/Little Caesars/Italian Coffee — three
   phone calls to find the operator group; pitch as Tier 1.
4. One **Chedraui** one-pager via the store gerente (flagship attempt; capped
   effort).
5. Seed (free, this week, from DENUE coords): Chedraui ×2, Aurrera ×5,
   Coppel main branches, banks on centro — as plain destination pins, not
   sponsor pins, so the map reads complete. *(Product note: these go in the
   places layer — already done via DENUE search — NOT as sponsor logos;
   sponsor pins stay paid-only per the pin policy.)*
6. Sales-time budget: **≤20% of field time on Tier 1 corporate-flavored
   targets; 0% on Tier 2/3** beyond the capped Chedraui attempt.
