## Sensitivity notes

- **Sponsorship churn higher than modeled**: If renewal drops to 60%, Year 2 Phase A
  revenue falls ~20%. The other phases compensate partially because they're independent.
- **Salesperson bottleneck**: All projections assume 1 full-time salesperson. Adding a
  second in month 7 accelerates Phases A-B by ~60% (two people covering different
  routes/neighborhoods).
- **Pricing power**: If the map achieves 10,000+ DAU by month 12, Phase C (Smart Search)
  pricing can double — the value of featured placement scales with audience.
- **Build delay risk**: Every month a phase slips is a month of that revenue missing.
  Phase B-D are the highest-ROI builds and should be prioritized in that order.
