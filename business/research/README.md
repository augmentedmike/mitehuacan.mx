# Research & Strategy — Index

The strategy corpus for MiTehuacán, in reading order. Later docs supersede earlier
ones where noted; **09 fixed the phase order, and 10–12 (July 2026) are the current
canonical growth + monetization thinking.**

## The arc in one line

> Give the map away (transport, live) → seed supply via QR (directory) → turn on the
> **viral loop** (fiesta invitations) → monetize **sales-closed, prepaid, digitally
> collected** (referral primitive: sponsorship + pages + boosts + referrals) → repeat the
> playbook per section. **Supply before demand before money.** Growth is organic/free;
> monetization is a human salesperson with digital collection — see
> [`../../financials/revenue-model-of-record.md`](../../financials/revenue-model-of-record.md).

## The two hardest open questions (both pilot measurements, not spreadsheet cells)

1. **Free→paid conversion** with no sales push (boost/Página conversion, re-up rate).
2. **The invitation k-factor** — does the fiesta loop actually compound (guest→host)?

Everything else is architecture and can be built; these two are measured in the
pilot (PRD-phase3 §11, phase2-revenue-capture §6).
