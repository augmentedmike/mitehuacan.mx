## 1. Why the old ordering is invalid

Three documents number the phases three different ways, and all three were written
on an assumption that no longer holds.

| Slot | `04-roadmap.md` | `05-marketplace-monetization.md` | `financials/` (A–G) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Transportation | Transportation **+ Route Sponsorships** | **A** Route Sponsorships (mo 1) |
| 2 | Route Sponsorships | Business Directory | **B** Business Directory (mo 4) |
| 3 | Business Directory | Smart Search | C Smart Search (mo 7) |
| 4 | Smart Search | Services Marketplace | D **Fiestas** (mo 10) |
| 5 | Services Marketplace | Fiestas | E Home Services (mo 13) |
| 6 | Community | Tianguis | F Tianguis (mo 16) |
| 7 | Fiestas | — | G Employment + Community (mo 19) |

**The shared, now-broken assumption:** one full-time salesperson closing route
sponsorships. That salesperson is ~60% of all projected revenue and the engine
that funds every later phase (see `06-phase-revenue-projections.md` and
`z-master-combined.md`). Our own Puebla adversarial review
(`07-adversarial-review-from-puebla.md`) says closing a digital sponsorship in
this market takes **3–5 relationship visits** and self-serve paid adoption is
"near zero."

**Remove the salesperson and the consequence is structural, not cosmetic:**

- **Route Sponsorships cannot lead.** It is the single most sales-dependent product
  we have — cold, relationship-gated, one-at-a-time. With no one to do the closing,
  it cannot be Phase 2. It does not disappear; it becomes a *self-serve upsell*
  layered on later, after demand is proven.
- **Every objection in the adversarial review is a *sales* objection.** No
  subscription culture, 3–5× slower sales, family decision-making, cash payment
  friction — all of these bite hardest at the moment a human tries to close a deal.
  A free, self-serve, QR-driven onboarding sidesteps all of them.
- **What already works with zero sales is the combi QR loop.** Transportation grows
  organically because riders scan a sticker and the map is immediately useful. That
  is a proven, zero-marketing acquisition mechanic. The correct strategy is to
  **extend that exact mechanic to businesses** — a printable QR they scan to add
  themselves — rather than invent a sales funnel we cannot staff.

The reorder below is therefore not a re-numbering. It is a change of engine: from a
**sales funnel** to an **organic, freemium, QR-seeded marketplace flywheel.**

## 2. The canonical order

> **Transport (live) → Fiesta Vendor Directory (free) → Fiestas (free) →
> Self-serve monetization (last).** Supply before demand before money.

| # | Phase | What it is | Growth mechanic | Money? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Transportation** *(live)* | Combi route map + trip planning | Combi QR stickers → organic riders | Free |
| **2** | **Fiesta Vendor Directory** | Business directory **seeded around fiesta-service categories**, self-onboarded | **Printable QR handed to businesses** → they add themselves | Free |
| **3** | **Fiestas** | Free consumer fiesta-planning tool (invites, RSVPs, needs list) | Viral: invitations spread person-to-person; pulls in non-transport users | Free for hosts |
| **4** | **Self-serve monetization** | Paid "Recomendado" / featured / priority-lead for vendors already getting free fiesta leads | WhatsApp + QR + OXXO/SPEI → auto-publish (tech *is* the salesperson) | **First revenue** |
| 5+ | Smart Search, Home Services, Tianguis, Employment/Community | Reuse the same directory + QR + self-serve rails | Same flywheel, new categories | Self-serve |

### The flywheel in one paragraph

Riders already arrive free via combi QR. We hand businesses a **printable QR** that
lets them add themselves to a **free** directory — seeded first with the categories
a fiesta needs (catering, pastel/repostería, DJ/música, foto/video, decoración,
mobiliario y carpas, salón de eventos, meseros, seguridad, flores, mariachi). We
then launch **Fiestas**, a free consumer tool where a host plans a party and states
what they need — which generates **qualified demand** the directory vendors can see.
Only *after* vendors are receiving **free** leads do we introduce paid upgrades
(be the "Recomendado," appear first, get priority leads), sold **self-serve** through
a WhatsApp + QR payment flow that replaces the salesperson with software. Every phase
after that reuses the same rails.

## 3. Two ordering decisions this locks in

### 3.1 Transportation is Phase 1 on its own; Directory is Phase 2

The marketplace-strategy doc bundled Transportation + Route Sponsorships as one
phase. Under the no-sales model that bundle makes no sense: transportation is a
*free organic engine*, sponsorships are *paid and sales-dependent*. They are
opposite kinds of thing and belong in different slots. Transportation stands alone
as the live foundation; the next thing we *build* is the free directory.

### 3.2 Fiestas moves up — from 7th to 3rd

The narrative roadmap buried Fiestas at Phase 7. That was wrong for two reasons:

1. **Fiestas is the demand engine the whole model needs.** It is the one product
   where the free incumbent (WhatsApp) is *weakest exactly at the point of value* —
   a host knows 2 caterers, not the best 10 with photos, prices, and reviews. It is
   inherently viral (invitations spread), seasonally resilient (cumpleaños, XV,
   bodas, bautizos, graduaciones happen year-round), and it is the only thing that
   gives a business a concrete reason to *want* our QR ("get catering leads") that a
   plain listing never does.
2. **The financial model already agreed** — it launched Fiestas 4th (Phase D,
   month 10), and its adversarial-adjusted revenue ($200–250K) is roughly *double*
   Home Services, Tianguis, and Employment combined. We are moving it one slot
   earlier still, because in the organic model Fiestas is not a late add-on — it is
   the reason the directory has value.

**Dependency, stated plainly:** Fiestas is useless without vendor supply, and the
directory has weak pull without Fiestas demand. This is the classic marketplace
chicken-and-egg. We break it with the **QR handout**: businesses self-onboard for
free (low friction, no salesperson) *because* the pitch is "get fiesta leads," and
the transport user base gives Fiestas its first hosts. Supply and demand are seeded
in the same motion.

## 5. One-line summary

> Old plan: hire a salesperson to close sponsorships, then add features.
> New plan: give the map away, let businesses add themselves via QR, make fiestas
> the reason they want in, and only charge — self-serve — once free leads are already
> flowing. **Supply before demand before money.**
