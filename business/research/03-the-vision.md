The Vision for mitehuacan.mx
More Than a Bus Route Website
mitehuacan.mx is building the digital marketplace for Tehuacán.
Today, residents struggle to answer simple questions:
• Which bus do I take?
• Where can I find a good dentist?
• Which mechanic has good reviews?
• What restaurants are near my destination?
• How do I get there without a car?
Our mission is to answer all of these questions in one place.
Public transportation is the foundation of the platform—not the final product.

Digital Real Estate
Every route is valuable.
Every stop is valuable.
Every neighborhood is valuable.
Businesses don't simply purchase advertising.
They reserve digital real estate where future customers are already traveling.
Just as a physical storefront on a busy street is more valuable than one on a side road, sponsorship
locations within mitehuacan.mx increase in value as more people use the platform.

Sponsorship Tiers
Each route contains a limited number of sponsorship opportunities.
For example, Route 23 may offer:

This creates scarcity, encourages early adoption, and provides businesses with meaningful differentiation.

Verified Reviews
Customer trust is built through transparency.
Users will be able to leave verified reviews, rate businesses, upload photos, and share their experiences.
Business owners can respond publicly, resolve concerns, and demonstrate excellent customer service.
The goal is to create the most trusted local review platform in Tehuacán.

A Platform Built for Tehuacán
Global platforms serve the entire world.
mitehuacan.mx serves one city exceptionally well.
Every feature is designed around how people in Tehuacán actually travel, shop, work, and live.
Instead of competing with Google, Facebook, or Instagram, mitehuacan.mx complements them by solving
a problem they were never designed to solve:
Helping local residents discover trusted businesses they can easily reach using public transportation.
As the platform grows, each sponsored route, featured category, and premium placement becomes
increasingly valuable digital real estate. Businesses that establish their presence early will benefit from
greater visibility, stronger customer recognition, and priority access to the most sought-after locations as
the marketplace matures.
