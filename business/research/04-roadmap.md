mitehuacan.mx Roadmap
Building the Digital Homepage of Tehuacán
Our Vision
Our goal is not to build a bus route website.
Our goal is to build the digital homepage of Tehuacán—the first place residents think of when they need
to find information, discover local businesses, connect with their community, or navigate the city.
We are not trying to compete with Google, Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok on a global scale.
We are building something those platforms were never designed to be: a single, trusted platform focused
entirely on the people, businesses, and daily life of Tehuacán.
The strategy is simple:
Build in phases.
Allow each phase to create users, data, trust, and revenue that make the next phase easier to launch.

Phase 1 – Public Transportation
The first product is public transportation.
Residents need a better way to:
• Find bus routes
• Locate bus stops
• Plan trips
• Discover landmarks
• Navigate the city
This solves a real, everyday problem for thousands of people.
It also creates something even more valuable:
Daily users.
Once people begin relying on mitehuacan.mx to get around the city, we have earned their attention and
trust.

Phase 2 – Route Sponsorships
Once people are using the platform regularly, local businesses gain a new opportunity.
Businesses can sponsor locations along routes, appear near relevant bus stops, and reach customers
already traveling through their neighborhoods.
This transforms the platform from a public service into a sustainable business.
Revenue from sponsorships funds continued development while creating measurable value for local
businesses.

Phase 3 – Local Business Directory
With businesses already joining as sponsors, the next step is to expand their presence.
Every business receives a rich profile including:

• Business information
• Opening hours
• Phone and WhatsApp
• Promotions
• Services
• Directions
• Reviews
• Owner responses
The platform evolves from showing businesses on a route to helping residents discover the best businesses
in Tehuacán.

The Flywheel
Every phase strengthens the next.
• Better transportation tools attract more users.
• More users attract more businesses.
• More businesses improve local search.
• Better local search increases daily usage.
• Higher daily usage makes sponsorships more valuable.
• Sponsorship revenue funds new features.
• New features attract more residents and businesses.
Each success reinforces the next, creating a self-sustaining ecosystem that becomes increasingly valuable
for everyone who lives, works, or visits Tehuacán.
