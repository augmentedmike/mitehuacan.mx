The Business Case for Sponsoring mitehuacan.mx
Why Should a Business Sponsor a Route?
Every business owner asks the same question:
"If I spend 500 or 1,000 pesos every month, what do I get?"
The answer is simple.
You get discovered by people who are already traveling near your business.
Unlike newspaper ads, radio, or Facebook advertising, mitehuacan.mx reaches people who are physically on
their way through your neighborhood.
Instead of paying to advertise to everyone, businesses reach potential customers at the moment they are
deciding where to stop, eat, shop, or buy.

Example
Imagine someone opens mitehuacan.mx to find Route 23 – San Isidro.
They immediately see:
• The route map
• Every stop
• Nearby landmarks
• Nearby businesses
• Sponsored businesses
• Walking distance
• Business hours
• Special offers (optional)
The business is not interrupting the user.
The business is helping them.

Why This Is Different
Traditional advertising asks:
"Will someone remember my business later?"
mitehuacan.mx asks:
"Can we help someone find your business while they're already nearby?"
That is a fundamentally different value proposition.
