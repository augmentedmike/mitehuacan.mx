# Seed-then-Monetize — The Per-Section Go-to-Market Playbook

*2026-07-24. The repeatable motion for launching **and** monetizing every feature /
section: seed it with free, credible local **partnerships** (anchor tenants) to
build "street cred" and fill the shelf, then flip *new* entrants to paid once the
section has proven pull. This is the GTM sequence that sits underneath the
monetization mechanism in
[`11-storefront-freemium-monetization.md`](11-storefront-freemium-monetization.md)
and the trust rule in [`../../PRD-phase3-fiestas.md`](../../PRD-phase3-fiestas.md) §7b.

It supersedes the "aspirational house placeholder logos (OXXO/bank)" seeding idea in
[`../../PRD-revenue.md`](../../PRD-revenue.md) §3 — real partners beat fake logos:
they give **earned** credibility, referrals, and warm monetization relationships that
placeholder logos cannot.*

---

## 1. The idea, stated

For each feature/section (fiesta recommendations, positioning, sponsorship,
referrals, deals, …):

1. **Seed with free partnerships.** Recruit 2–3 credible, beloved local businesses
   into the "featured / promoted" slots at **no cost**.
2. **Low approval bar.** Because it's free, a **store manager's yes** is enough — no
   owner, no family decision, no cost objection.
3. **This is our street cred.** Real local brands make the section look alive,
   legitimate, and worth using — for residents *and* for the next business.
4. **Flip to paid once the section has ~2–3 partners in it.** New entrants pay
   (positioning, sponsorship, referrals, etc.); the section becomes a market.

Correct instinct. Three refinements (§4) turn it from good to durable.

## 2. Why it works (the startup logic)

- **It solves the empty-shelf / cold-start problem.** A two-sided surface that
  launches empty is dead on arrival. Seeded supply means it never looks empty — the
  single most important thing for early credibility.
- **It's the anchor-tenant playbook.** Malls give big, beloved brands free or
  subsidized rent because their presence draws the paying smaller tenants. The free
  anchors here are *the product that sells the paid slots.*
- **It sidesteps the decision-maker wall — the review's hardest constraint.** The
  Puebla review says a paid close takes **3–5 visits** because of owner/family
  decision-making. A **free** featured placement needs only a manager's yes: no cost,
  no dueño, no "lo tengo que consultar." You capture the logo and content **now**,
  and the later paid ask is a **warm inside-sale** to a business already getting
  value — exactly the warm state Phase 2 was designed to manufacture, achieved for
  free.
- **It generates the usage data that makes the paid pitch real.** Seeded anchors
  receive real leads/views; that data is the ROI story you point slot #4's buyer at.

## 3. The repeatable motion (run this per section)

```
1. SEED     Recruit 2–3 credible, beloved local businesses into featured slots — FREE.
            Manager-yes is enough. No cost, no owner sign-off, no contract.
                    │
2. ALIVE    The section now looks legitimate + populated. Residents use it;
            anchors receive real leads/views. Usage data accrues.
                    │
3. PROVE    Section demonstrates PULL (leads / foot traffic / views the anchors feel).
                    │
4. FLIP     NEW entrants pay for position (prepaid season, OXXO/SPEI, self-serve).
            Scarcity is now real: the category exists, is desirable, slots are limited.
                    │
5. ANCHOR   The 2–3 seeds are GRANDFATHERED free/founding-rate forever — they become
            permanent social proof + referral sources, not churned takeaways.
```

Same motion for every section ("same rails, new categories," roadmap Phase 5+), now
with a go-to-market attached.

## 4. Three refinements that make it durable

### 4.1 Grandfather the seed partners — permanently

**Do not flip your first 2–3 anchors from free to paid.** A takeaway breeds
resentment and can turn your best local references into detractors. Instead, lock
them at **"socio fundador — gratis / tarifa fundador para siempre."** This converts
the seed cost into a **permanent asset**:

- They are the **logos on the slide** for every paid pitch.
- They are the **social proof** that makes the section desirable.
- They are **referral engines** — compadrazgo is the acquisition channel
  (marketing-plan: *"el compadre sells better than we do"*). A happy free anchor
  introduces the paying newcomers.

New entrants pay; anchors stay free *as* the credibility that sells them. The seed is
not a discount you claw back — it is marketing spend that keeps paying.

### 4.2 Gate the flip on demonstrated *pull*, not just the count of 3

"2–3 partners in a section" is the **necessary** condition (the shelf looks full).
It is **not sufficient** to charge. You can only monetize once the section
**demonstrably drives value** — leads, foot traffic, views the anchors can feel.

- Seed to **fill the shelf AND generate usage data.**
- Flip to paid only when there is a **value story to point at** (slot #4's buyer sees
  what the anchors are getting).
- Flip too early and nobody bites — there's no proven ROI yet, and a failed paid
  launch is harder to restart than a patient one.

**Count is the trigger to *look* ready; pull is the trigger to *charge*.**

### 4.3 The flip is a prepaid season, not a subscription

The paid tier the section flips into must be the **one-shot prepaid / positioning**
product, **never a monthly subscription** (the cash-economy constraint, killed three
times in the research; full mechanism in
[`11-storefront-freemium-monetization.md`](11-storefront-freemium-monetization.md)
§2–3). Same up-trend, cash-native mechanism, no collections.

## 5. Consistency with the trust rule

This reinforces — does not conflict with — the earned-vs-paid separation in
[`../../PRD-phase3-fiestas.md`](../../PRD-phase3-fiestas.md) §7b:

- **Seed with genuinely credible, beloved businesses.** Then "featured" *means*
  something and the seeding **builds** the earned-trust layer (real anchors), rather
  than laundering junk into a trusted slot.
- **The later paid product sells labeled position** ("Destacado"/promoted), not the
  unbuyable trust signal (reviews, verified, completed-party count).
- The danger to avoid: seeding with low-quality businesses just to hit the count of
  3. That spends the street cred instead of building it. Anchors must be *anchors.*

## 6. Use the free period as decision-maker reconnaissance

The manager-yes that gets you the free seed is not the person who will approve the
later paid ask. Use the free relationship to **map and warm the actual
decision-maker** (dueño/family) before the flip — so when a section monetizes, the
paid conversation is with the right person and already warm. Free seeding is also
intelligence-gathering for the eventual sale.

## 7. Scarcity is the pricing engine

The 2–3 free anchors are not just decoration — they **manufacture the scarcity that
prices slot #4**. Sequence:

```
Seed category with free credible anchors → category now has social proof + is desirable
        → position in it becomes scarce (limited featured slots per category/zona)
        → newcomers pay for the scarce, now-proven position
```

This is the vision doc's "limited sponsorship opportunities per route / digital real
estate" logic, made bottom-up and self-serve. You don't announce scarcity; you
**create** it by filling the desirable slots first, for free, with names people trust.

## 8. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Seeding with weak businesses to hit the count | Anchors must be genuinely beloved/credible — quality over speed; a junk anchor spends cred |
| Flipping to paid before there's proven pull | Gate on demonstrated value, not the count of 3 (§4.2) |
| Seed partners resent being asked to pay later | Grandfather them free/founding forever (§4.1) — never claw back |
| "Subscription" framing creeps back in | Flip = prepaid season/positioning, never monthly (§4.3) |
| Manager-yes ≠ paying decision-maker | Use the free period to map/warm the dueño (§6) |
| Free forever cannibalizes revenue | Only the 2–3 anchors per section are free; scarcity + newcomers carry the revenue |

## 9. One-line summary

> Seed every section with 2–3 free, genuinely beloved local partners (manager-yes,
> no cost — sidestepping the owner-decision wall) to fill the shelf and build street
> cred; let their real leads generate the ROI story; then flip *new* entrants to a
> **prepaid** positioning product once the section shows real pull — while
> **grandfathering the anchors free forever** so the seed cost becomes permanent
> social proof and referral fuel. The free anchors are the product that sells the
> paid slots. **Occupancy first, scarcity next, money last — per section, on repeat.**
