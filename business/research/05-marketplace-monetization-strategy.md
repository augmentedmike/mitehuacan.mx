Marketplace Monetization Strategy
Every Feature Creates Demand. Businesses Pay to Meet It.
Our philosophy is simple:
Residents use mitehuacan.mx for free.
Their activity creates demand.
Businesses pay to respond to that demand.
This keeps the platform valuable to the community while creating multiple recurring revenue streams.

Phase 5 – Fiestas
Residents create fiestas for free.
They invite guests, manage RSVPs, and organize their celebration.
During setup they answer questions such as:
• Do you need catering?
• Do you need a cake?
• Do you need a photographer?
• Do you need a DJ?
• Do you need tables and chairs?
• Do you need decorations?
• Do you need a venue?
• Do you need security?
• Do you need bartending?
• Do you need cleanup services?
The resident is not paying for referrals.
They are simply asking for help.
Service providers pay to receive those qualified opportunities.
Revenue:
• Featured vendors
• Exclusive vendor placement
• Lead fees
• Monthly vendor subscriptions
• Premium vendor profiles
Instead of advertising to everyone, vendors are connected directly with people who are actively planning an
event.

The Long-Term Vision
mitehuacan.mx is not an advertising platform.
It is a local demand marketplace.
Residents use the platform because it helps them navigate daily life.
Every search, every route, every review, every event, every fiesta, every recommendation request, and every
service inquiry creates demand.
Businesses don't pay simply to be seen.
They pay to connect with residents at the exact moment those residents are looking for what they offer.
That alignment creates a platform where community value and business value grow together.
The more useful the platform becomes for residents, the more valuable it becomes for local businesses—
and the stronger the network effect becomes for everyone.
