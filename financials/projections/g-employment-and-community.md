## Reconciliation from adversarial review [→ full review](challenges/phase-g--employment--community.md)

| Assumption | Projection | Adversarial challenge | Adjusted range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid job board demand | 48 accounts by month 24 at $450/mo avg | Multiple "empleos en Tehuacán" Facebook groups with 50K+ members dominate job listings — all free. A paid job board needs to offer something free Facebook groups don't. [1] | 20-30 accounts |
| $800/mo employer subscription | Unlimited postings | Employers in Tehuacán post jobs for free in Facebook groups. Paying $800/mo for a subscription requires dramaticly better candidate quality, which is unproven. | $300-400/mo avg |
| Paid community event promotion | $200/event | Events are promoted free via Facebook events and WhatsApp forwards — ubiquitous, well-understood, zero cost. [2] | Very low adoption. |
| Month 19 launch | Phase D-F revenue funds it | This phase is the furthest out and most speculative. By month 19, the platform will have real data to validate or invalidate the whole model. | Treat as speculative. |

**Net effect:** 20-30 accounts by month 24. Avg $300-400/mo. Year 2 revenue $25K-35K (not $57K). Most speculative phase — validate prior phases first.

[1] Adversarial difference #4: Facebook is not a competitor for this phase — it is the entrenched incumbent with zero cost.
[2] Adversarial difference #9: WhatsApp + Facebook Events are the existing event promotion infrastructure. Displacing them requires dramatically better value.

## Sensitivity notes

- **Sponsorship churn higher than modeled**: If renewal drops to 60%, Year 2 Phase A
  revenue falls ~20%. The other phases compensate partially because they're independent.
- **Salesperson bottleneck**: All projections assume 1 full-time salesperson. Adding a
  second in month 7 accelerates Phases A-B by ~60% (two people covering different
  routes/neighborhoods).
- **Pricing power**: If the map achieves 10,000+ DAU by month 12, Phase C (Smart Search)
  pricing can double — the value of featured placement scales with audience.
- **Build delay risk**: Every month a phase slips is a month of that revenue missing.
  Phase B-D are the highest-ROI builds and should be prioritized in that order.
