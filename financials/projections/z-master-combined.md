- **Build delay**: Each month a phase slips = that month's projected revenue missing. Prioritize B-D.

### What the adversarial review agrees with

| Finding | Status |
|---|---|
| Unit economics work at volume | ✅ Real |

| 25-35% cold close rate | ❌ 3-5x too high |
| $500/mo average Year 1 | ❌ Founding rate is $250-400/mo |
| 80% renewal rate | ❌ 50-60% more likely |
| Self-serve digital adoption | ❌ Requires hand-holding in this market |

### Structural differences that affect every phase

The adversarial review identified 10 structural differences between Tehuacán and a major US city that affect all projections. Every phase is impacted by at least 3 of these:

1. **Cash economy** — Payment collection is operations, not billing [all phases]
2. **No subscription culture** — Advertising is bought one-shot [phases A, B, C, D, E]
3. **3-5x slower sales** — Relationship building is mandatory [phases A, B, F]
4. **Facebook is the internet** — Standalone adoption is capped without FB strategy [all phases]
5. **Informal economy majority** — 55-60% of businesses fear digital visibility [phases B, E, F]
6. **Family decision-making** — Single-visit closes are rare [phases A, B, F]
7. **Seasonal cash flow** — January and September dips are predictable [phases A, D, E]
8. **Cheap known alternatives** — Perifoneo, radio, flyers are understood [phases A, C, F, G]
9. **WhatsApp is the platform** — Your app is a tool they manage for you [phases D, G]
10. **Physical logistics** — Heat + distance cap sales output [phase A]
