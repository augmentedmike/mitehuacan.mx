## Reconciliation from adversarial review [→ full review](challenges/phase-a--route-sponsorships.md)

| Assumption | Projection | Adversarial challenge | Adjusted range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month 1 new sponsors | 3 | Zero relationship capital. First close takes 4-8 weeks. | 0-1 |
| Cold close rate | 25-35% [1] | 3-5% first visit; 5-10% cold; 20-25% warm referral [2] | 5-10% cold |
| Price per location | $500/mo [3] | Founding rate is $250-400/mo per MARKETING.md | $325/mo avg |
| Sponsor value (4 locs) | $2,000/mo | At $325/mo founding rate: $1,300/mo | $1,300-1,600/mo |
| Year 2 renewal rate | 80% [4] | Comparable Mexican micro-business products see 50-60% Year 1 renewal | 50-60% |
| Salesperson output | 250 visits/mo | Heat, distance, relationship-building cap output [5] | 120-150 visits/mo |

**Net effect:** Year 1 gross $750K-850K (vs. $1,312K projected). Year 2 gross $1.8M-2.2M (vs. $3,740K projected).

[1] MARKETING.md §7 assumes 10-minute cold close. Adversarial: in Tehuacán, first visit is for relationship, not close.
[2] Referral-based selling (introduced by a known contact) is the norm in Mexican small-business culture.
[3] The Year One projection uses $500/mo (standard rate) from month 1. MARKETING.md §6 sets founding rate at $250-400/mo for the first 100 sponsors.
[4] 80% renewal is optimistic even for US SaaS. Mexican micro-business subscription products historically churn at 40-60%/year.
[5] 35°C heat + spread-out commercial corridors + relationship time per visit = fewer daily visits than a US salesperson in a climate-controlled office park.
