## Reconciliation from adversarial review [→ full review](challenges/phase-f--tianguis--local-commerce.md)

| Assumption | Projection | Adversarial challenge | Adjusted range |
|---|---|---|---|
| $250/mo avg per vendor | 100 vendors by month 24 | Tianguis vendors are among the most informal businesses. Many lack RFC, bank accounts, or smartphones. Paying $50-100/day for a physical stall — $200-300/mo for a digital profile they don't understand is a very hard sell. [1] | $150-200/mo avg |
| Digital discovery value prop | "Customers can find you on the map" | Tianguis traffic is walk-by, not digital. A vendor's customer base comes from physical location, not online discovery. The value prop for this segment is weak. [2] | Minimal organic demand |
| Self-serve / online sales | Feature is "lightweight" | Tianguis vendors are not reachable through digital channels. Requires physical presence at the tianguis with a different salesperson and approach. | Requires field sales team |

**Net effect:** 30-50 vendors by month 24 (not 100). Average price $150-200/mo (not $250). Year 2 revenue $40K-60K (not $101K). Requires distinct sales channel targeting tianguis vendors in person.

[1] Adversarial difference #5: Informal economy participation means these vendors have the most to lose (SAT attention) and the least to gain (digital discovery doesn't drive their foot traffic).
[2] Adversarial difference #8: Existing advertising alternatives (announcements at the tianguis, radio, word of mouth) are free or cheap and already work for this demographic.
