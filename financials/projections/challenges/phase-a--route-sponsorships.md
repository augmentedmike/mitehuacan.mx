# Adversarial Review

## Phase A — Route Sponsorships

#### Assertion: 3 new sponsors in month 1, ramping to 13 by month 6

**Challenge:** Month 1 includes zero time for relationship building. The
salesperson needs to learn the routes, identify which combis run them, find
the owners, build rapport, and then sell. Realistically, month 1 produces
0-1 sponsors (if the salesperson starts with the owner's own network).
Month 2 produces 1-2. Month 3 produces 2-3. The ramp is 3 months delayed.

**Adjusted estimate:** Month 1: 0-1. Month 2: 1-2. Month 3: 2-3. Month 4: 4. 
Not 3-5-7 for months 1-3.

#### Assertion: 25-35% close rate

**Challenge:** This is the single most dangerous assumption in the entire
projection. In a market where trust must be earned over multiple visits,
where the decision maker is often absent, and where the product is a new
category (digital sponsorship), a 25-35% close rate is achievable only if
you count *introductions from trusted sources*, not cold visits. The cold
visit close rate in Tehuacán for a new digital product is closer to 5-10%.

**Adjusted estimate:** 5-10% cold close. 20-25% on warm leads (referral,
second visit, introduced).

#### Assertion: $500/mo per location, 4 locations per sponsor = $2,000/mo average

**Challenge:** The business/marketing-plan.md pricing says founding rate is $250-400/mo per
location. The projection uses $500/mo — the *standard* rate — from month 1.
If the founding rate ($250-400) is actually charged for the first 100 sponsors,
the average is $325/mo, not $500/mo. With 4 locations, that's $1,300/mo per
sponsor, not $2,000. This alone cuts Phase A revenue by 35%.

**Adjusted estimate:** $325/mo average first year → $1,300/sponsor → 35% less
revenue than projected.

#### Assertion: 80% renewal rate Year 2

**Challenge:** Subscription churn in Mexico for new digital products aimed at
micro-businesses runs 40-60% in year one across comparable products I have
seen (directory listings, local advertising platforms). The 80% renewal
assumption would be optimistic for a US SaaS product. For a Mexican micro-
business product, it is unrealistic.

**Adjusted estimate:** 50-60% renewal. Year 2 active sponsors peak at 100-110,
not 181.

#### Net effect on Phase A:

| Metric | Projected | Adjusted |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 gross | $1,312,000 | $750,000-850,000 |
| Year 2 gross | $3,740,000 | $1,800,000-2,200,000 |
| Active sponsors (mo 24) | 181 | 100-120 |
