# Adversarial Review

## Phase E — Home Services Marketplace

#### Assertion: 2,000+ registered service professionals; 95 providers by month 24

**Challenge:** The home services marketplace model (Thumbtack, TaskRabbit)
faces a specific problem in Mexico: trust is personal. When a plumber is
needed, the homeowner asks their network — vecinos, familia, el compadre.
They do not search a platform. The platform needs to overcome the trust
barrier for both sides: homeowners need to trust that providers are vetted,
and providers need to trust that leads are real and worth paying for.

**Challenge on $400/mo membership:** A plumber in Tehuacán might make
$8,000-15,000/mo in revenue. Paying $400/mo (3-5% of revenue) for leads
that are unproven is risky from their perspective. Pay-per-lead at $100
feels safer but requires the platform to deliver enough volume to matter.

**Challenge on timing:** Month 13 assumes the platform has 2,000+ DAU by
then. If Phase A underperforms (see my adjusted estimates), user base grows
slower, which means fewer service requests, which means less value for
providers, which means lower retention.

**Adjusted estimate:** 50-60 providers by month 24 (not 95). Year 2 revenue:
$120,000-150,000 (not $232,000).
