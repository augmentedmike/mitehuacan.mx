# Adversarial Review

## Phase F — Tianguis & Local Commerce

#### Assertion: 10+ established tianguis. 100 vendors by month 24.

**Challenge:** Tianguis vendors are among the most informal businesses in
Mexico. Many do not have RFC, bank accounts, or smartphones. Paying $200-300/mo
for a digital profile when they pay $50-100/day for their physical stall is
a hard math problem. The value prop ("people can find you on the map") means
little when the vendor's customer base is walk-by traffic at the tianguis,
not digital discovery.

**Challenge on adoption:** Tianguis vendors are not browsing the internet
for business tools. Reaching them requires physical presence at the tianguis,
which means a different sales approach — and likely a different salesperson
who speaks the language of mercado vendors, not the language of tech.

**Adjusted estimate:** 30-50 vendors by month 24 (not 100). Average price
closer to $150/mo (not $250). Year 2 revenue: $40,000-60,000 (not $101,000).
