# Adversarial Review

## Phase D — Fiestas & Events Marketplace

#### Assertion: 75,000 households, 1% host a fiesta per year = 750 events

**Challenge:** The number of households hosting a *planned fiesta* (birthday,
quinceañera, boda, etc.) is probably higher than 1%. But the question is
whether those hosts will use a new digital platform to organize it. Currently,
fiesta planning in Tehuacán happens via WhatsApp groups, word of mouth, and
known vendors. The platform needs to offer something dramatically better than
a WhatsApp group. The US analogy (Evite, Paperless Post) does not apply
because WhatsApp already does this for free and everyone already has it.

**Challenge on vendor willingness to pay $500/mo:** A DJ in Tehuacán might
charge $2,000-4,000 per event. Paying $500/mo ($6,000/yr) for leads means
they need 2-3 additional events per year just to break even. If the platform
delivers 1-2 leads per month in early stages, the math works. If it delivers
1 lead per quarter, it does not. Vendor retention depends entirely on lead
quality, which depends on host adoption, which is uncertain.

**No mention of physical fiesta logistics:** Many fiestas in Tehuacán happen
in the home, at a salon de eventos, or in the street (with permits). The
platform needs to handle location logistics, which vary enormously.

**Adjusted estimate:** 40-50 vendors by month 24 (not 92). Year 2 revenue:
$200,000-250,000 (not $455,800).
