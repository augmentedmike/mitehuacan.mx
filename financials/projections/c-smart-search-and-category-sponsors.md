## Phase C — Smart Search & Category Sponsors

*Launches month 7. Requires search-by-category feature (not built yet).*

**Product:** Businesses pay for featured placement when users search by category
("dentista," "restaurante," "farmacia"). Category exclusivity ("La Farmacia Recomendada
de la Ruta 15") at premium pricing.

**Price:** 
- Featured listing: $500/mo per category per route
- Category exclusivity: $1,500/mo per route (only one)
- Commission: none (digital upsell)

**Inventory:** 15 top categories × 20 highest-traffic routes = 300 slots. Exclusivity:
15 categories × 20 routes = 300 exclusive slots. Total addressable slots: 600.

## Reconciliation from adversarial review [→ full review](challenges/phase-c--smart-search--category-sponsors.md)

| Assumption | Projection | Adversarial challenge | Adjusted range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 600 addressable slots | 15 categories × 20 routes × 2 tiers | Many routes serve residential areas with only 2-3 of these categories present. Theoretical max ≠ real inventory. [1] | 200-300 real slots |
| $1,500/mo for exclusivity | Category exclusivity premium | A pharmacy making $3-5K/day paying $1,500/mo (1-2% of revenue) for a badge on an unproven platform is a very hard sell. [2] | $400-600 avg |
| 165 slots by month 24 | 28% of inventory filled | Requires search-by-category feature to exist AND residents to change behavior from "which route?" to "which pharmacy on my route?" | 60-80 slots |
| No dedicated sales | Upsell during sponsor visits | If Phase A underperforms (see Phase A reconciliation), there are fewer visits to upsell during. | Dependencies compound |

**Net effect:** 60-80 slots by month 24 (not 165). Year 2 revenue $300K-400K (not $807K). Feature dependency delays launch if Phase A revenue doesn't fund it.

[1] Adversarial difference: US-market inventory models overapply to Mexican cities where commercial corridors are concentrated and residential areas are sparse for most categories.
[2] Advertising alternatives (perifoneo, radio) are cheap, understood, and one-shot. A monthly premium for digital exclusivity is an unproven concept.
