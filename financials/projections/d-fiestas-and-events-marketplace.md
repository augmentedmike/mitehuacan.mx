## Phase D — Fiestas & Events Marketplace

*Launches month 10. Fiesta planning (free for hosts) + vendor marketplace.*

**Product:** Hosts create fiestas for free, get asked about needs (catering, cake, DJ,
photography, decorations, venue, security, tables/chairs). Vendors pay for leads or
subscription.

**Price:**
- Vendor subscription: $500/mo (unlimited leads within their category + route)
- Lead fee: $100/qualified lead (lower commitment)
- Featured vendor: $800/mo (appears first)

**Market:** ~75,000 households in Tehuacán. Even 1% hosting a fiesta per year =
750 events. Each event needs 3-5 vendors on average.

## Reconciliation from adversarial review [→ full review](challenges/phase-d--fiestas--events-marketplace.md)

| Assumption | Projection | Adversarial challenge | Adjusted range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fiesta planning via new platform | 750 events/year from 1% of 75K households | Fiesta planning in Tehuacán happens via WhatsApp groups + word of mouth. A new platform must be better than WhatsApp groups (which everyone already has). [1] | Adoption slower |
| Vendor subscription $500/mo | 92 vendors by month 24 | A DJ charging $2-4K/event needs 2-3 additional events/year from the platform just to break even on $500/mo. Lead quality must be proven before vendors pay. [2] | 40-50 vendors |
| Host → vendor lead math | 750 events × 3-5 vendors = 2,250-3,750 leads/yr | Only works if >1% of hosts use the platform. Chicken-and-egg: vendors won't pay without hosts, hosts won't join without vendors. | Requires Phase A-C user base |

**Net effect:** 40-50 vendors by month 24 (not 92). Year 2 revenue $200K-250K (not $455.8K). Heavy chicken-and-egg dependency on Phase A user adoption.

[1] Adversarial difference #9: WhatsApp is the platform in Mexico. Any new social/event tool needs to be meaningfully better than a WhatsApp group, not just different.
[2] Adversarial difference #7: Cash flow is seasonal. Vendors in lean months (January, September) may suspend subscriptions regardless of lead quality.
